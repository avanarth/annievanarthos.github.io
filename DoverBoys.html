Dover Boys: Why is it important?

Dover Boys remains iconic and inspirational to this day for a couple of reasons. Firstly, the short is funny and well-made, perfect for animation students to observe how to draw animated expressions with great comedic timing. Secondly, Dover Boys is one of the few cartoon shorts directed by famous animation director Chuck Jones that remain in the public domain to this day. Dover Boys can also be classified as the "first modern cartoon" for its useage of limited animation.

Animation stuents and fans take inspiration from the short to this day, cementing it's artistic legacy.
