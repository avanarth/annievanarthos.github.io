What is the Public Domain?

The public domain is a legal framework where, after a certain period of time, (95 years later for coroparte art, life +70 years for art owned by an author's estate), artistic works and IP's owned by copyright holders becomes null and void; therefore available to the genral public.

Comic books, comics strips and cartoons before 1930 are included in the public domain! In addition, if a copyright of a property laspes, it enters the public domain even if it isn't old enough.

In addition to novels, plays and other more traditional forms of art, comics and cartoons have become PD due to lasped coyprights. Some examples of icomic PD cartoons are:

  • Miss Fury
  • Buck Rodgers(1929 strips)
  • The Dover Boys

Fun Fact! Casper the friendly ghost is in the public domain!
