A Feminist Hero?

For most comic book fans, Wonder Woman stands out as the first woman superhero to be the star of their own comic line. While this is true in regards to the two main comic companies, DC and Marvel, but other smaller superhero creators were creating heros that spoke to modern women. Among pre-Wonder Woman women superheros, why did I chose Miss Fury; why is Miss Fury unique?

What Makes Miss Fury Unique?

  1. Miss Fury was the first superhero comic to be written by a women author
  2. Miss Fury rarely puts on her super hero costume; she prefers to fight crime and deal with her issues through plain-clothes deduction
  3. Miss Fury becomes a single mother during the series, and the comic protrays her struggles realisticly
  4. Places the role of "femme fatala" from a womens perspective

Miss Fury's Impact:

In a era where femme fatales such as Catwoman enter the Superhero genre, mostly focused on giving young boys idols to gawk at, the presentation o Miss Fury as a smart, confident trickster hero stands strong. Many women superheroes would sprout become inspired from this famous comic, inspiring women for decades to come!
