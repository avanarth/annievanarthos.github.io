Buck Rodgers: Birth of Opera

Buck Rodgers's Legacy

Buck Rodgers first burst onto the scene in newspaper comic form during the beginings of the Great Depression. Despite this, the visual language, sci-fi concepts and technology of Rodger's world remain staples of 20th-21st century science fiction media. In particular, Buck Rodgers became a staple of the "Space Opera" sci-fi genre which started to gain traction in the 1910's.

Fun Fact!: Rodgers vs Dodgers

Buck Rodgers is the primary point of parody for the famous Looney Tunes short plus 2000's cartoons series "Duck Dodgers", staring Daffy Duck. The cartoon targeted the pulp series because its style became so emblematic of where the genre had arrived by the 1950's.

Space Opera: Types and Categories

Space Operas can be divided into categories witch various famous works of sci-fi setting the stage of tone of the subgenres. Space Opera are defined as sci-fi works which resemble fantasy stories or pulp novels more than hard sci-fi serials; in addition, there is a focus on inter-planetary conflict, the lives of ordinary people/paramiliary forces and melodrama.
